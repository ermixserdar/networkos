NetworkOS – Privacy Policy

Version 1.0, effective 2026-09-08

Short version: NetworkOS stores everything on your device. No account, no server, no tracking, no ads, no analytics SDK.

1. Data we collect

We collect nothing. NetworkOS has no backend server and sends no data to us or third parties. All content you enter — contacts, notes, relationships, follow-ups, commitments, meetings, companies, tags, birthdays — is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device only.

2. Device permissions (all optional)

  • Contacts: used only when you tap Import, to copy people from iOS Contacts into your local database. Nothing leaves the phone. You can deny permission and enter contacts manually.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used only if you enable App Lock in More > Security. Authentication happens on-device. No biometric data ever leaves the device.
  • Notifications: if enabled, reminders (follow-ups, birthdays) are scheduled locally on-device. No push server is used.
  • Files / Share sheet: backup export writes a file to your device storage only when you explicitly tap Export.

3. Backup & export

  • Encrypted backup (.networkos file): encrypted on-device with a key stored in iOS SecureStore / Keychain. Only someone with your device can restore it.
  • CSV export: explicitly marked in-app as not encrypted — anyone with the file can read it.

4. No tracking, no third-party SDKs

No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or cross-app tracking SDKs are included in v1.0.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data lives as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes the local database. There is nothing to request from us because we hold no copy.

6. Children

The app is a general productivity tool with no objectionable content (rated 4+). No data is collected from anyone, including children.

7. Contact

Questions: support@networkos.app

Gizlilik Politikası (Özet – Türkçe)

Kısa versiyon: NetworkOS tüm verileri cihazınızda saklar. Hesap yok, sunucu yok, takip yok, reklam yok.

  • Veri toplama: Hiçbir veri toplanmaz, hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez. Tüm kişiler, notlar ve hatırlatmalar yalnızca cihazınızdaki şifreli veritabanında durur.
  • İzinler (tamamı isteğe bağlı): Kişiler (yalnızca İçe Aktar'a dokununca), Face ID (yalnızca uygulama kilidi açıksa), bildirimler (yalnızca cihaz içi hatırlatmalar), dosya paylaşımı (yalnızca siz dışa aktarırsanız).
  • Yedekleme: Şifreli yedek yalnızca cihazınızda açılır. CSV dışa aktarımı şifreli değildir.
  • Silme: Uygulamayı silmek tüm verileri siler. Bizde kopya yoktur.
  • İletişim: support@networkos.app
